## Sources

The methodology, hazard screening list, asset categories and term set follow
these FEMA publications and tools, as referenced in the protocol:

- **FEMA IS-393, Introduction to Hazard Mitigation** (Emergency Management
  Institute independent study course), Lesson 3, Risk Assessment: the
  four-step method (identify hazards, profile hazard events, inventory
  assets, estimate losses), the hazard inventory list, critical-facility
  categories and the composite risk summary.
- **FEMA 386-2, State and Local Mitigation Planning How-To Guide:
  Understanding Your Risks, Identifying Hazards and Estimating Losses.**
- **Hazus-MH** (FEMA's loss estimation software), https://www.fema.gov/hazus,
  named as the recommended follow-on tool for quantitative loss estimates.

The protocol also directs the user to public data sources for hazard
profiling (FEMA National Risk Index, FEMA Flood Map Service Center / FIRM,
USGS, NOAA Storm Events Database and National Hurricane Center, NWS, USFS
wildfire risk data). It does not reproduce their data.

FEMA publications are works of the U.S. Government and are in the public
domain in the United States. Their use here does not imply FEMA endorsement
of this skill or of Preppr.ai.
