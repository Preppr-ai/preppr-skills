## Sources

The protocol applies methodology, standardized impact language and
standardized capability target language from these FEMA publications:

- **Comprehensive Preparedness Guide (CPG) 201: Threat and Hazard
  Identification and Risk Assessment (THIRA) and Stakeholder Preparedness
  Review (SPR) Guide**, 3rd edition, FEMA, May 2018. The three-step THIRA
  methodology, the consequence-over-likelihood emphasis, and the
  standardized impacts and standardized capability targets.
- **2019 National Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment
  (THIRA): Overview and Methodology**, FEMA, 2019. The national application
  of the three steps, scenario context descriptions (Appendix B), and the
  Plausible Concurrent Operations approach (Appendix D).
- **National Preparedness Goal**, 2nd edition, FEMA / DHS, 2015. The core
  capabilities and the Cross-Cutting, Response and Recovery mission areas the
  targets are organized under.
- **Community Lifelines** construct, FEMA. The lifeline categories used to
  align each capability target.

The protocol also names, as places to look for data, the FEMA National Risk
Index, National Planning Scenarios, Federal Interagency Operational Plans
(FIOPs), National Planning Frameworks, HAZUS-MH, and public data from CISA,
USGS, NOAA and CDC. It does not reproduce their content.

FEMA, DHS and other U.S. federal agency publications are works of the U.S.
Government and are in the public domain in the United States

skill or of Preppr.ai.
