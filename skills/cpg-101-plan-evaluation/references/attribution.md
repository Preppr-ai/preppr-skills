## Sources

The evaluation criteria follow these U.S. Government publications, as
referenced in the protocol:

- **FEMA Comprehensive Preparedness Guide (CPG) 101, Developing and
  Maintaining Emergency Operations Plans**: the standard the plan is
  evaluated against, including planning principles, plan structure (basic
  plan, functional and support annexes, threat/hazard-specific appendices)
  and the essential basic plan elements. The protocol does not name an
  edition; use the edition current for your jurisdiction and say which one
  you applied.
- **National Preparedness Goal** and its core capabilities: named as the
  integration target for capabilities-based planning.
- **National Incident Management System (NIMS)**, including the Incident
  Command System (ICS): the command, resource management and
  communications criteria.
- **FEMA Threat and Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (THIRA)**
  process: the capability targets and gaps the plan should reflect.
- **Emergency Support Functions (ESFs)**: the annex structure the protocol
  expects.

The protocol also names PHEP and EMAP only as alternative frameworks a user
may choose in the follow-up options; they are not sources for the CPG 101
evaluation.

FEMA publications are works of the U.S. Government and are in the public
domain in the United States. Their use here does not imply endorsement of
this skill or of Preppr.ai by FEMA or any other agency.
