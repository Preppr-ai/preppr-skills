## Sources

The methodology, capability target language, rating approach and funding
alignment follow these U.S. Government publications and programs, as
referenced in the protocol:

- **FEMA Comprehensive Preparedness Guide (CPG) 201, Threat and Hazard
  Identification and Risk Assessment (THIRA) and Stakeholder Preparedness
  Review (SPR) Guide** (3rd Edition, 2018): the THIRA / SPR process, the 22
  standardized capability targets and their (#) / (time) target language,
  mission areas, and the POETE (Planning, Organization, Equipment,
  Training, Exercises) capability elements.
- **FEMA Community Lifelines** guidance: the primary lifeline assigned to
  each capability target.
- **FEMA National Preparedness Report (NPR)**: named as a benchmark source
  for capability data.
- **National Incident Management System (NIMS) Resource Typing**
  definitions: named as the benchmark for equipment and team capability.
- **Federal grant program guidance** for the programs in the alignment
  table: Emergency Management Performance Grant (EMPG); Homeland Security
  Grant Program (HSGP), including the State Homeland Security Program
  (SHSP) and Urban Area Security Initiative (UASI); Hazard Mitigation
  Assistance (HMA), including the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP),
  Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities (BRIC) and Flood
  Mitigation Assistance (FMA); HUD Community Development Block Grant —
  Disaster Recovery (CDBG-DR); HHS / ASPR Hospital Preparedness Program
  (HPP); and CDC Public Health Emergency Preparedness (PHEP). The protocol
  summarizes each program's general fit; it does not reproduce program
  notices, and current eligibility, match and deadlines must be checked in
  the program's current Notice of Funding Opportunity.

FEMA publications, and the HUD, HHS and CDC program materials named above,
are works of the U.S. Government and are in the public domain in the United
States. Their use here does not imply endorsement of this skill or of
Preppr.ai by FEMA or any other agency.
