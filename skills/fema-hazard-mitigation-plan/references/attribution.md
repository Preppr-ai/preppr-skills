## Sources

The methodology, action categories, evaluation criteria and plan-content
requirements follow these regulations, FEMA publications and tools, as
referenced in the protocol:

- **44 CFR Part 201.6, Local Mitigation Plans**: plan-content, public
  participation, multi-jurisdictional action, adoption and update
  requirements.
- **Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000 (DMA 2000)**: the statutory basis for
  local mitigation plans and the 5-year update cycle.
- **FEMA IS-393, Introduction to Hazard Mitigation** (Emergency Management
  Institute independent study course), Lesson 4: the four-step mitigation
  plan-development method, problem statements, goals and objectives
  examples, the six mitigation action categories and STAPLE(E); Lesson 5 is
  cited for SBA disaster loans.
- **FEMA 386-3, State and Local Mitigation Planning How-To Guide:
  Developing the Mitigation Plan.**
- **FEMA 386-4, State and Local Mitigation Planning How-To Guide: Bringing
  the Plan to Life.**
- **FEMA Benefit-Cost Analysis (BCA) Toolkit**, named as the follow-on tool
  for the Economic dimension of STAPLE(E).
- **FEMA mitigation best-practice resources** named as sources of candidate
  actions: the FEMA Mitigation Action Portfolio, FEMA P-2181 and FEMA's
  Mitigation Best Practices.
- **Funding programs** named in the grant-alignment table: FEMA Hazard
  Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP), Building Resilient Infrastructure and
  Communities (BRIC), Flood Mitigation Assistance (FMA), FEMA Public
  Assistance (Sections 404 / 406 mitigation), SBA Disaster Loans, and HUD
  CDBG-DR / CDBG-MIT. Program availability, cycles and cost-share terms
  change; confirm current terms with the State Hazard Mitigation Officer or
  the current funding notice.

The protocol also points to public sources for hazard-specific mitigation
practice (USGS, USACE, NOAA). It does not reproduce their content.

FEMA publications and federal regulations are works of the U.S. Government
and are in the public domain in the United States. Their use here does not
imply FEMA endorsement of this skill or of Preppr.ai.
